      About

      The Artisan Story & Process

      Handcrafted pearl and gemstone jewellery, found across Indonesia and shared with the world.

      Our journey

      Inspired by our travels across Bali and many regions of Indonesia, we discovered extraordinary craftsmanship and made it our mission to share it with the world.

      We travel together, meet the artisans, personally select each pearl and gemstone, and source carefully chosen materials from different places across Indonesia.

      We then transform these pearls and stones into unique and beautiful pieces of jewelry. The story of this process is our own story.

      Rose quartz symbolizes self-love, fluorite represents a clear mind, and pearl shell evokes tranquility. Each product features its signature stone and its meaning, so you can choose jewelry for both its symbolism and its beauty.

      Our artisans

      Every piece is handcrafted by our team of artisans in the workshop, from the stones and pearls we choose. Their work draws on skilled craft from all over the archipelago, reaching far beyond traditional Balinese jewellery, so our pieces reflect a rich mosaic of Indonesian heritage.

      Almost everything is finished on titanium, in a white or a yellow tone, with an extension chain so one bracelet fits most wrists.

      The craft, in its own words

      The catalogue names each technique and design in Indonesian. This is what they mean.

      Polos
      Plain. A single clean row of pearls.
      Susun
      Stacked. Two or three strands worn as one.
      Simpul
      Knotted. Strands tied into a centre knot.
      Rajut
      Woven. Stones worked into a braided cord.
      Serut
      Drawstring. A sliding clasp that adjusts to the wrist.
      Kerikil
      Gravel. Small tumbled chips of stone.
      Model anggur
      Grape design. Chips clustered along a chain.
      Model bunga
      Flower design. Pearls gathered into blossoms.
      Model lampion
      Lantern design. A rounded pearl centrepiece.
      Model kacamata
      Figure-eight design. Two loops, like a pair of glasses.

      Policies

      Authenticity & Returns

      What we guarantee about every piece, and what to do if something is not right.

      Authenticity & certificates

      • Genuine materials

        Every piece is made with genuine pearls and natural stones, bought from trusted artisans and suppliers across Indonesia.

      • Jade grading

        Each jade piece is named with its grade. Type A jade is natural and untreated. Type C jadeite has been colour-treated.

      • Certificates on request

        A gemological certificate can be arranged for eligible pieces. Ask us on WhatsApp or by email before you order. Lab fees and extra time may apply, depending on the lab.

      • Natural variation

        Small differences in colour, grain and texture are part of natural, handmade jewellery and a sign of its authentic origin.

      Returns & exchanges

      • 14 days

        Start a return or exchange within 14 days of receiving your order.

      • Condition

        The piece must be unworn and in its original condition, with its packaging, pouch and any certificate.

      • Final sale

        Custom, personalised or resized pieces, and pieces that show wear, damage or changes after delivery.

      • How to return

        1. Message us on WhatsApp or email info@yarecraft.com with your order details and the reason.
        2. Once approved, we send you return instructions.
        3. Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the piece arrived damaged or incorrect.

      • Refunds

        Once we receive and check the piece, we refund your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Original shipping fees are not refunded.
